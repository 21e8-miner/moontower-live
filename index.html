Arithmetic on stated assumptions · no EDV market data

Data: Moontower API. Snapshots are end-of-day (16:00 ET) with intraday updates on trading days; a GitHub Action probes Moontower five times each trading day and re-pulls only when the snapshot changed. Vol figures are annualized percent. VRP = 100·ln(IV30 / RV30). Scenario values, probabilities and half-lives are Black-Scholes with zero drift and the vol re-implied from each call's mid; they are a ruler, not a forecast. The duration translation (1% of TLT ≈ 6.25 bp, EDV ≈ 1.5× TLT) is an assumption, stated on the cards. Nothing here is investment advice.
