Data: Moontower API. Snapshots are end-of-day (16:00 ET) with intraday updates on trading days; a GitHub Action probes Moontower five times each trading day and re-pulls only when the snapshot changed. Vol figures are annualized percent. VRP = 100·ln(IV30 / RV30). "Cheap" here means low implied volatility, relative to the other wing, to at-the-money, and to TLT's own history. Nothing here is investment advice.
